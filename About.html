My Hobbies

Coding

My interest in coding started as a curiosity about how websites, applications, and software actually work behind the scenes, but it has gradually grown into one of my favourite hobbies. Being a Computer Science student has given me an opportunity to explore different programming languages and technologies, particularly Python, Java, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. What fascinates me most about coding is the ability to turn an idea into something functional, whether it is building a website, developing a simple application, or solving a problem through logic. I enjoy experimenting with new technologies, troubleshooting errors, and challenging myself to find better ways of writing code. Although the process can sometimes be frustrating, especially when dealing with bugs that seem impossible to fix, the satisfaction of finally getting something to work makes it worthwhile. For me, coding is more than just an academic skill; it is a creative outlet, a continuous learning experience, and a hobby that I hope to transform into a successful career in the tech industry.

Cinema

I've always been a huge movie nerd, but Marvel has always held a special place in my heart. Music

My music-listening journey began in 2014, when I found myself deeply immersed in music, largely influenced by my older cousin's taste and preference for hip-hop and trap. Those early years shaped my musical identity, with my first major deep dives revolving around Lil Wayne, Jason Derulo's Trumpets, and Nicki Minaj's energetic hits that dominated the scene back then. Over the years, however, my taste has evolved significantly. I've gradually moved away from the high-energy sounds of my early years towards slower, smoother, and more relaxing tones, eventually discovering Don Toliver, whom I consider my favourite artist of the 21st century. Nevertheless, my appreciation for music remains diverse. I'm still fascinated by Kanye West's incredible versatility and ability to experiment across different sounds, while I deeply respect Kendrick Lamar's commitment to preserving and representing the roots and culture of Black hip-hop. From 2014 to now, my musical journey has been one of growth, exploration, and discovering new sounds without ever losing touch with the influences that started it all.
